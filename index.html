Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Call or text (213) 375-8658 — evening appointments Monday through Friday, plus Saturdays.

What's the difference between microblading and ombré brows?

Microblading uses hair-like strokes for a natural look; ombré brows are softly shaded like makeup. Combo brows blend both techniques.

I'm nervous about pain — what should I expect?

Clients consistently say microblading is nothing to be scared of — Q makes sure you're comfortable every step of the way.

What are your hours?
